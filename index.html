THE VIVA, SIMPLIFIED

A better neighbour, one assignment at a time.

01 / Represent

A state assigns each waiting passenger to E1, E2, or E3. Start with the nearest available elevator.

02 / Explore

Change one passenger’s elevator to create a neighbour. Estimate its total cost with the same movement rules.

03 / Improve

Choose the lowest-cost neighbour. Repeat only while cost decreases; stop at a local optimum.

cost = predicted wait + distance + 5 × reversals + 2 × queued load penalty

Hill Climbing can get stuck at a local optimum. A lower estimated cost does not guarantee that every individual metric improves. Capacity is unlimited for this simplified demo; onboard passengers keep their elevator.

THE VIVA, SIMPLIFIED

A better neighbour, one pickup group at a time.

01 / Represent

Group waiting passengers by pickup floor and direction. Identical waiting requests share one lift; after pickup, new arrivals are evaluated again. A state assigns each group to one of the selected elevators. Start with the nearest elevator.

02 / Explore

Change one pickup group’s elevator to create a neighbour. Estimate its total cost with the same movement rules.

03 / Improve

Choose the lowest-cost neighbour. Repeat only while cost decreases; stop at a local optimum.

cost = predicted wait + distance + 5 × reversals

Waiting counts passenger delay; distance counts movement; reversals discourage backtracking. The weight 5 is a simple design choice: one reversal costs as much as five extra floor moves or passenger-wait steps. It is not a measured physical constant. Unlimited capacity means no load penalty.

Hill Climbing can get stuck at a local optimum. A lower estimated cost does not guarantee that every individual metric improves. Capacity is unlimited for this simplified demo; onboard passengers keep their elevator.
